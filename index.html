About Savant
Built by someone who gets it.

Finance and technology are reshaping the world, yet most young people are never taught how to navigate them. Savant changes that.

We build AI powered tools that help the next generation understand personal finance, technology, and innovation — and use that knowledge to build real careers.

Savant was founded under YSJ Ventures with one mission: make financial education accessible to everyone who was left out of the conversation. Because your zip code, your school, or your background shouldn't determine your financial future.

Accessibility first
Finance shouldn't require a finance degree to understand. We translate complexity into clarity for everyone.
Built for Gen Z
Every tool is designed for young people entering the workforce and taking control of their money for the first time.
AI powered, human focused
We use AI to personalize the experience — so every user gets exactly what they need, when they need it.
Career driven
We don't just teach finance — we help you turn that knowledge into internships, certifications, and careers.
